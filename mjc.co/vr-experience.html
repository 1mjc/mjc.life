Academic co-op · Immersive prototype

VR Experience System

A memory-based VR concept: real places rebuilt as 3D environments, guided by an AI companion.

Type
Academic co-op project (a design prototype, not a clinical product)
My role
Creative systems design, technical research, experience design
Tools
Unreal Engine, Gaussian Splatting, conversational AI

The project

A concept for immersive, memory-based spaces. I built a system that turns Gaussian Splatting scans into realistic 3D environments in Unreal Engine, supported by an AI companion that guides the person through the experience.

The objective

Explore whether immersive storytelling can make memory feel inhabitable and support feel personal, designing for comfort and care rather than attention.

My role

Creative systems design, technical research and experience design.

Approach

  1. Photoreal environment reconstruction using Gaussian Splatting
  2. An AI companion with a backstory, memory and tone
  3. Configurable experience templates so a therapist or user can adjust a session

Output

  • A completed VR demo built in Unreal Engine
  • Sample experiences (pictured above)
  • The experience flow and template concept

Context

Academic co-op project. It's a design prototype, not a clinical product.

What it shows

Comfort working in Unreal Engine and with emerging tech such as Gaussian Splatting and conversational AI, and the ability to turn a complex technical idea into something people can step into. That's useful for immersive brand experiences and interactive campaigns.
