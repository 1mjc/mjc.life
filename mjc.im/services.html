Brand work

What I bring to a team.

I work out who a brand is, how it should look and feel, and how it shows up online. Then I make it: the identity, the materials, the pages and the content. I can own something start to finish, and I'm just as useful as one part of a bigger team.

Identity

Positioning, tone of voice, logos, type and colour systems, and the rules that keep them consistent.

Creative direction

Moodboards, campaign concepts and art direction that hold one look and feel across every touchpoint.

Digital presence

Websites and landing pages designed and hand-built, plus social systems that look like the brand, not a template.

Content and video

Short-form, launch pieces, music videos and VFX, from storyboard to final cut.

AI-assisted production

Faster concepting, more variations and small workflow automations, with a person making the calls on taste.

Why it works

One person, no handoffs

I can carry an idea from first sketch to final file, so I understand what happens before and after my part of a project.

Range, so one idea goes further

Brand, design, video, web and a little code. One concept can become the identity, the page and the content, which helps anywhere a campaign has to live in more than one place.

I build my own

Mangosalad and Visit Vice Versa weren't briefs; I started them. Building my own things taught me what makes people want to join in, and how to ship without being asked.

Where I can contribute

Creative development

Concepts, moodboards and campaign ideas, from first brief to a direction a team can run with.

Multimedia and content production

Video, motion and social assets, made quickly and on-brand.

Digital design and experiences

Websites, landing pages and interactive work, designed and built.

Open to part-time creative and marketing opportunities in Toronto and the GTA, and still happy to talk about freelance projects.

I've mostly worked independently. Now I'm looking for a team where I can contribute from day one and learn from people who've been doing this longer than I have.
