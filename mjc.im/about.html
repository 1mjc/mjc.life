About

A web, not a line.

I'm Matheus Camilleri, a multimedia designer in Vaughan and Toronto. I graduated from Multimedia Design and Development at Humber Polytechnic in 2025, and I've been building projects of my own since 2020.

How it connects

My work looks scattered until you zoom out. I paint, I make VFX and music videos, I build worlds, businesses and platforms. They're branches of the same thing: I like deciding how something should feel, then building everything it needs to feel that way.

Working together

I'm direct, I move quickly, and I take feedback well and run with it. Whether it's a role or a project, the first step is just saying hello.

The doorway

Brand work is the easiest way in: identity, creative direction and digital presence. It's what I'm best at, on a team or on a project of my own. See what that looks like, or wander the map for everything else.

If I had $22 million

I'd build a multi-studio creative ecosystem with shared infrastructure: a global content network through Mangosalad, decentralized grants and artist licensing through Artprojects, and community-powered landscapes through Good Dirt. I'd make replicable systems that make artists iconic, not just seen.
