## Why the music

A lot of my videos are cut to songs by Nettspend. His music puts me in a feeling and makes me daydream; there's a lot of emotion in it. He'll always be one of my favourite artists.

## What I make

- Art and music videos
- Ad video and short-form content
- VFX and compositing
- Launch pieces for brands and projects

## How I work

One person, idea to final cut. I come up with the concept, shoot it, cut it, build the effects and finish it. On a bigger shoot I'm just as happy being one part of a crew.
