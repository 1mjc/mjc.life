## Who made these

Khaleb Herrington is a separate identity from mjc, and from me. Where the name comes from stays a mystery.

## The work

I started painting in 2018 using only ink, and ink is still the heart of it, alongside digital pieces. Most pieces are environments or landscapes with little beings living in them: a world trying to evolve. It looks dark. But there's always a glimmer of hope in there, if you look hard enough.
